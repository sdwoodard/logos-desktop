# LogosDesktop application icon

logos-desktop.svg adapts the **Book Open Text** glyph from
[Lucide](https://lucide.dev/icons/book-open-text), by Lucide Icons and Contributors.
The glyph is distributed under the [ISC license](lucide-LICENSE.txt).

Source revision: `02eb9161b57917ccaa262f1bf070507cddde3b08`.
[Original SVG](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/02eb9161b57917ccaa262f1bf070507cddde3b08/icons/book-open-text.svg).

The adaptation preserves the glyph paths and adds a blue rounded-square background,
white strokes and application-sized placement. logos-desktop-icon.png is rendered
from this SVG master. The icon represents the independent LogosDesktop controller.
It is not a Logos corporate mark.
